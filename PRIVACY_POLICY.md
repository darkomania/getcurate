# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Curate does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

All data (your bookmarks and your settings) stays inside your own browser. Bookmarks are read locally through Chrome's bookmarks API and are never sent anywhere. Settings are stored using Chrome's storage API and, if you enable Chrome Sync, synced by Chrome across your own devices under your own Google account.

Curate contains no analytics, no tracking, and communicates with no external servers. The only external requests are to load site favicons and, when Google is the selected search engine, the Google logo image.
